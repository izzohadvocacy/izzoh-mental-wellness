Mental Wellness

Understanding & Supporting Mental Wellbeing

Mental wellness includes emotional wellbeing, relationships, coping, purpose, social connection and knowing when to seek help.

🌱

Healthy Routines

Encourage consistent sleep, movement, meaningful activities, rest and manageable daily routines.

💬

Talk About Feelings

Respectful conversations can reduce isolation and help concerns be recognized earlier.

🫶

Support Networks

Trusted people, professionals and community supports can strengthen resilience and connection.

🧘

Manage Stress

Planning, breaks, breathing exercises, problem-solving and healthy boundaries can support coping.

🔎

Know When to Seek Help

Persistent distress, major changes in functioning or safety concerns deserve timely professional attention.

📚

Learn & Reduce Stigma

Reliable information and compassionate language can help communities respond with dignity.

Emergency note: If someone is in immediate danger or experiencing a mental-health emergency, seek urgent medical or emergency assistance.
